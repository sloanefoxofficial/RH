# Sloane Fox — Original Classic Arcade Art Direction

## What the supplied examples make clear

The visual target is **not** simply low-resolution pixel art. It is a dense 16-bit arcade composition with a strong fight-space hierarchy. The combat plane has a rough, irregular, hand-painted texture; characters are comparatively large and grouped near the centre; scenery creates a deep layered frame; shadows and colour ramps make forms feel material; and the horizon is full of specific environment information.

Sloane Fox will use this as a broad genre direction only. It will not reproduce any particular game’s characters, scenery, layouts, UI, names, or individual art. All hero, existing minor-enemy, and boss assets remain unchanged.

## Original visual rules

| Area | Required original treatment |
| --- | --- |
| **Combat framing** | Move the active fight space higher and tighter. Keep heroes and enemies large enough to occupy the middle third of the screen. Use rough ground texture, contact shadows, and a shallow 3/4 lane. |
| **Ground** | Replace repeating brick-like stripes with irregular clusters of cracked asphalt, grit, leaves, puddles, embedded stones, paint remnants, roots, or cobbles appropriate to the stage. Use several colour ramps rather than a flat fill. |
| **Horizon** | Build uneven depth from original structures: ledges, broken walls, alleys, trees, railings, shop fronts, scaffolding, platform roofs, retaining walls, rooflines, poles, and distant silhouettes. |
| **Pixel material** | Use clustered highlights, darker inset shadows, chipped edges, and organic irregularity. Avoid evenly repeated windows, identical tree crowns, isolated rectangles, and broad unmodulated bands. |
| **Stage mood** | Use earthy/dusky city and park palettes: rusts, moss, brown-greys, dirty blue-green, sodium yellow, wet concrete, and occasional high-contrast danger colour. Keep the Super tiers as deliberate high-value accents. |
| **Activity** | Add independent scenery motion and timed hazards that are visually announced: buses, magpies, rain, drifting litter, platform signals, flashing signs, and foliage movement. These must not be copies of reference-game events. |

## Visual pass/fail check

A final screenshot should read as an **original late-1980s/early-1990s arcade lane brawler** before its title is read: dense textured floor, detailed layered surroundings, high material contrast, and large central combat. It must not look like a clean vector city, early-console abstraction, a collection of UI panels, or a direct copy of any reference image.
