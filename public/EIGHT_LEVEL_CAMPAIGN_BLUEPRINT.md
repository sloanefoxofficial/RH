# Sloane Fox — Eight-Level Arcade Campaign Blueprint

## Campaign rule

Every existing boss receives one **complete, long, self-contained level**. A level is not just a background. It must include a location reveal, nine escalating combat fronts, material-specific scenery, living world activity, breakables/rewards, one compact arcade event, a pressure finale, and a boss arena that feels like the place has been reached.

| Level | Existing boss | Full location | Route identity | Active world pressure | Compact arcade event | Boss arena |

| 1 | Anxiety | **Rainy Side Street** | Shops → bus shelter → alley mouth → closed pharmacy. | Timed bus in marked road lane; gusting paper; flickering lamp. | **Skateboard Rush:** jump onto a board, steer clear of cones for a short lane run, dismount safely. | Wet pharmacy forecourt with an awning, broken sign, streetlight, and curb-side traffic. |
| 2 | Depression | **Underground Station** | Concourse → ticket gates → platform → empty train carriage exterior. | Signal chime, platform draft, timed train arrival beyond safety line. | **Platform Dash:** fire a jammed turnstile, then jump the closing gap. | Last platform in blue-grey service light, with train doors and warning signal. |

## Long but fair front rhythm

Each stage uses the same total length but different visual dressing and event timing. The nine fronts use existing pressure types—Ruminator, Worry Spore, Self-Doubt Geist, Burden Imp—in changing combinations rather than multiplying a single enemy.

| Front | Gameplay role | Fairness rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Location reveal and mixed warm-up. | One or two pressure types; room to learn the stage lane. |
| 2 | Side-pincer encounter and first breakable. | Clear entry points; one health/power opportunity. |
| 3 | Ranged/depth pressure. | Avoidance lane remains visible. |
| 4 | Optional Support Runner or reward object. | Missing it costs only the bonus, never progress. |
| 5 | Stage-specific hazard warning. | Warning, timing window, and low-damage recovery state. |
| 6 | Relief cache and brief reset. | Health and power pickup after intensity. |
| 7 | The level’s signature compact event. | Finishes within roughly two seconds; no trapping state. |
| 8 | Pressure finale with breakable/risk decision. | Multiple approaches; no unavoidable collision. |
| 9 | Boss threshold wave and arena reveal. | Final power/health decision before boss warning. |

## Stage art rules

The scene plate is a base, not the whole presentation. Every active level needs its own background/midground/foreground depth, material-specific floor, backdrop animation, event prop, and a boss-arena dressing state. Existing heroes, minor enemies, and bosses are never redrawn or replaced.
