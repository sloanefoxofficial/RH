HOW TO STAND

INSTRUCTIONS

You don't fight people — you fight the struggles. Clear each area, beat its boss, and survive the Death gauntlet at the end. Grab glowing ★ to charge your Super and ❤ to heal.

MOVE — D-pad / WASD  (P2: Arrows) — walk any direction
JUMP — JUMP button / G  (P2: /)
FIRE — FIRE button / F  (P2: .) — hold to CHARGE a heavy shot
JUMP + FIRE — press FIRE while in the air = JUMP KICK (lunges at the nearest enemy)
SUPER — SP button / T  (P2: ,) — 3 tiers: ⅓ small blast · ⅔ heavy · FULL = MAX POWER wipes the screen
LIVES — you have 3. Lose them all and it's Game Over (you can Continue).
