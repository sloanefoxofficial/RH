🔥 Around this fire — Full Final Rules

  • Pull up a chair — come and go as you please
  • No fixing, no unsolicited advice — just listening first. Lived-experience tips & what’s worked for you are always welcome, when they’re asked for.
  • What's said here stays here — never share names, stories, screenshots, or details outside this space
  • Silent is fine — you don't have to speak
  • Everyone equal — no one runs it, no one leads
  • Anonymous welcome — no real name needed
  • You can use your name, a nickname, or stay "Bloke" — whatever feels right
  • Avatar, photo, or no image at all — your call, no pressure
  • Voice or text — whatever feels right. Voice recommended.
  • Speech bubbles show who's speaking — clear, quiet, no confusion
  • Be respectful — no abuse, no put-downs, no judgment. Everyone’s carrying something you can’t see.
  • Look out for each other — people might not be who they say they are. Keep personal details private, don’t share contact info publicly.
  • No hate, no politics, no pushing agendas — just real blokes sharing real stuff.
  • Share what’s worked for you — your own experience is the most valuable thing you can give. No “shoulds”, just “this is what helped me”.
  • Want to connect one-on-one safely? Use the VIDEO button at the top — pick who you want to talk to, and they have to accept. No need to share phone numbers or personal details — it’s all built right into the app for your safety.
  • If someone’s struggling — be kind. If you’re worried about someone, reach out privately or flag it. We’re all looking out for each other.
