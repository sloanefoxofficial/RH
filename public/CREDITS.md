# Sloane Fox — Stand Together Credits and Environment Licences

## Project artwork and gameplay

The **Male Fox**, **Female Fox**, existing minor-enemy assets, and existing boss assets remain the supplied project artwork. They have not been edited, reskinned, replaced, moved, or incorporated into any third-party asset pack.

The eight-level campaign layout, front progression, stage-specific hazards, arcade moments, boss-arena dressing, original route material details, original Level 1 rainy side street, game code, and supporting procedural scene additions are part of the Sloane Fox project build.
