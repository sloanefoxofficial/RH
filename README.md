# Resources section — clean redesign

This package is based on the plan-engagement version of the app. Replace `App.jsx` and `images.js`, then deploy the complete project.

The Hub now has a single `Resources` card in `Stay Connected`, styled with a food/shopping-bag icon. The page is organised into Immediate support; Food and meals; Housing and essentials; Legal, money and bills; Addiction recovery; Family, children and youth; Health and wellbeing; and Stay safe.

The supplied material was edited to remove emojis, repeated AI-style commentary, and uncertain claims. Food includes the Ask Izzy 2165 search, Miller Community Cafe’s no-questions-asked outreach, and Foodbank Australia. Recovery includes Western Sydney Local Health District and Odyssey House NSW. The page includes an in-page table of contents and the floating button returns to that table of contents while on Resources; elsewhere it retains its normal behaviour. Stay Safe is no longer a Toolkit group, but its existing in-app tool remains reachable from Resources.

External service hours, eligibility, fees, and availability should be checked before publication because they can change.
